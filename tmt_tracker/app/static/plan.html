Printers and their setups

Each printer works through its setups in this order. A setup prints plates of its filament and groups (none picked = any); changing nozzle size between setups is a swap you do at a plate change. For example: TPU on a 0.6, then the straps on a 0.4, then PETG on the 0.6.
